The idealized slab

the analytical stress-perturbation solution

This is the paper’s analytical solution (Eq. 2) for the depth-averaged stress perturbation in a slab of ice of thickness H and surface slope α sliding over a circular free-slip patch of radius R. The parameter c0H/η sets the basal slipperiness outside the patch, where c0 is the basal traction coefficient and η is the ice viscosity. The perturbation decays over a length √(4ηH/c0) (Eq. 3), so a more slippery bed (smaller c0H/η) transmits stress farther. Stresses are in kPa. The color scale is fixed per field and does not change with the sliders.
