The idealized slab

the analytical stress-perturbation solution

The paper’s analytical solution (Eq. 2) for the depth-averaged stress perturbation in a slab of ice (thickness H, surface slope α) sliding over a circular free-slip patch (radius R), with dimensionless basal slipperiness c0H/η outside the patch — c0 the basal traction coefficient, η the ice viscosity. The perturbation decays over √(4ηH/c0) (Eq. 3): a more slippery bed (smaller c0H/η) transmits stress farther. Stresses in kPa; the colour scale is fixed per field — it does not change with the sliders.
